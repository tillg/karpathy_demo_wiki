# Clip: Lünersee and Douglasshütte

*Notes from a social-media clip posted 2026-08-12, rewritten in English. Figures approximate.*

- Douglasshütte on the shore of the Lünersee, Vorarlberg — dreamlike setting, reachable entirely without a car.
- Dormitory beds at a modest alpine-club member rate instead of a five-star hotel.
- Up on foot or with the Lünerseebahn.
- Comments: the walk round the lake is easy; climb a little higher for the view from above; the next day you can go
  on to the Totalphütte.
