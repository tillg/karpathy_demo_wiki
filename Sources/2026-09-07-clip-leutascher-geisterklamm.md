# Clip: Leutascher Geisterklamm

*Notes from a social-media clip posted 2026-09-07, rewritten in English. Figures approximate.*

- The Wasserfallsteig is the oldest and most spectacular part of the Leutascher Geisterklamm, a gorge between
  Mittenwald (Bavaria) and Leutasch (Tyrol).
- A narrow, historic wooden walkway leads just above the water deep into the gorge and ends at a 23 m waterfall.
- The walkway is about 200 m long; sturdy shoes and a head for heights needed.
- Open May to mid-October, daily during the day (closed on Fridays from September); closed in winter. Entrance fee,
  cash only.
- Not allowed on the walkways: dogs, prams, wheelchairs, bikes.
