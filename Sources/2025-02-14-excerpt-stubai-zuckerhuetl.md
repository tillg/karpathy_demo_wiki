# Stubai Alps: Zuckerhütl (excerpt)

*Clipped from a ski-touring guide (example.org). Figures approximate.*

**Zuckerhütl (3,507 m)**, the highest summit of the Stubai Alps. Start at the Mutterbergalm (1,721 m); most parties
use the glacier lifts to save the first 1,000 m and tour from the Eisgrat area over the Pfaffenferner to the
Pfaffenjoch, then up the north-east flank (ski depot, last metres on foot). Glacier, north-east facing, season
March to May. Moderate (WS+).
