# DAV: "Natürlich auf Tour" — the ski-touring campaign for nature

*Source: https://www.alpenverein.de/verband/natur-und-klima/naturvertraeglicher-bergsport/natuerlich-auf-tour (accessed 2026-10-02). Summarised in own words.*

"Natürlich auf Tour" (#natürlichauftour) is the DAV campaign for nature-friendly ski and snowshoe touring in the
Bavarian Alps. Its aim is to protect wildlife in winter while keeping the mountains open for sport.

**History.** It grew out of a DAV working group called "Skibergsteigen umweltfreundlich" (environmentally friendly ski
mountaineering), started in 1995 and soon run together with the Bavarian environment ministry and the Bavarian State
Office for the Environment. The "Natürlich auf Tour" name was added in 2015 to make the results better known.

**Routes and zones.** There are recommended routes for about 500 ski routes and variants, covering roughly 180
frequently climbed summits, plus 225 forest and wildlife protection zones that tourers should stay out of completely.

**Species.** The focus is on the winter habitats of capercaillie and black grouse, which the page describes as
threatened with extinction in the region.

**Behaviour.** Stick to the recommended routes, avoid the protection zones and leave wildlife its winter quiet.

**Where to find it.** The routes and zones appear on the DAV's "BY" series of maps, on signs on site, in ski-touring
guidebooks carrying the campaign label, on alpenvereinaktiv.com and in OpenStreetMap.

**Legal status.** Participation is voluntary — there are no bans or fines — but the page notes that official bans
could follow if the zones are deliberately ignored.
