# Trip report: Hirschberg, 18 January 2025 (own notes)

Classic short tour above Kreuth near the Tegernsee. Start at Scharling (about 770 m), 900 m of ascent through the
forest and over the Hirschberghaus. Good powder on the north side after a cold week. These low Prealps tours only
work in mid-winter; by March there's usually no snow in the forest any more.
