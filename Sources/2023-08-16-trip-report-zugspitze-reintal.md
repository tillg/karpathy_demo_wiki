# Trip report: Zugspitze via the Reintal, 14–15 August 2023 (own notes)

Long hike from Garmisch-Partenkirchen (about 720 m) through the Partnachklamm and the Reintal. Night at the
Knorrhütte (2,051 m). Day two over the Zugspitzplatt and the last via-ferrata-like section to the summit (2,962 m).
Took the cable car down. About 2,200 m of ascent; fit, but not technical.
