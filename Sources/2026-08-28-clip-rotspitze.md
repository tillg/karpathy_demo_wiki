# Clip: Rotspitze, Rofan

*Notes from a social-media clip posted 2026-08-28, rewritten in English. Figures approximate.*

- One of the most beautiful ridge walks in the Alps: Rotspitze, Rofan mountains, Austria.
- Cable car up to the Erfurter Hütte, then a loop of about 4 hours, 8 km and 550 m.
- Black (difficult) trail with two short sections where you pull yourself up on steel cables — easy if you are fine
  with heights.
- Quiet even on a sunny weekend; the Achensee below and the whole Karwendel in front.
- A good day trip from Munich or Innsbruck; swim in the Achensee afterwards.
- One comment disagrees: a hard hike with scrambling.
