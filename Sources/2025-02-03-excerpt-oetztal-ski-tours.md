# Ötztal Alps: classic glacier ski tours from Vent (excerpt)

*Clipped from a ski-touring guide (example.org). Figures approximate.*

Vent (1,895 m) at the end of the Venter Tal is the base for some of the highest ski tours in Austria. The season
on the glaciers starts in March, when the huts open, and usually runs into mid-May. All tours below cross
glaciers: rope, harness and crevasse rescue kit are mandatory.

**Wildspitze (3,768 m)** — Austria's second-highest summit. Two days: up to the Breslauer Hütte (2,844 m), then over
the Mitterkarferner to the ski depot below the summit ridge and on foot to the top. South-east facing in the upper
part, so it softens early on sunny spring days. About 1,900 m of ascent in total.

**Similaun (3,599 m)** — from Vent through the Niedertal to the Martin-Busch-Hütte (2,501 m), then over the
Niederjochferner. North-west facing glacier, holds good snow well into May. Moderate (WS).

**Fluchtkogel (3,500 m)** — an easy glacier summit, popular as the first tour of a hut week. From the Vernagthütte
(2,755 m) over the Guslarferner. North-east facing. Easy (L–WS).
