# Clip: Rifugio Passo Santner

*Notes from a social-media clip posted 2026-09-18, rewritten in English. Figures approximate.*

- Probably the most controversial hut in the Dolomites: modern and conspicuous, on an edge of the
  Sciliar–Catinaccio (Schlern–Rosengarten) massif, with stunning views straight from the dining room.
- Worth a stop for the view, ideally a night (it is often full).
- Two ways up: the Passo Santner via ferrata (not a hard one), or from the Fassa valley past the Preuss, Vajolet and
  Re Alberto huts.
