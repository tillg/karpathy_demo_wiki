# Clip: Mittenwalder Höhenweg

*Notes from a social-media clip posted 2026-08-04, rewritten in English. Figures approximate.*

- Location: Karwendel above Mittenwald. Start at the top station of the Karwendelbahn, finish at the valley
  station in Mittenwald.
- Time: about 6–7 hours in total — along the ridge, then down via the Brunnstein hut to the valley.
- Grade: A/B, an easy to moderate, well-protected via ferrata. Sure feet, a head for heights and via-ferrata kit
  required.
- Best time: July to October; in early summer often old snow on the approach and descent.
