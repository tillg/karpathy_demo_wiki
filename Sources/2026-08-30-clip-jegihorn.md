# Clip: Jegihorn via ferrata

*Notes from a social-media clip posted 2026-08-30, rewritten in English. Figures approximate.*

- Called the highest via ferrata in Switzerland: the Jegihorn route above Saas-Grund leads to the summit at
  3,206 m.
- Features: a suspension bridge, an exposed arête, several vertical ladders and steep sections.
- Difficulty: K4.
