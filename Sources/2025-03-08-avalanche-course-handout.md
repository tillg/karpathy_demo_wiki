# Avalanche course: handout (own notes)

Notes from the two-day avalanche course.

- **Danger scale** 1 (low) to 5 (very high). Most accidents happen at levels 2 and 3. Read the whole bulletin: danger
  level, problem (new snow, wind slab, persistent weak layer, wet snow, gliding snow), aspects and altitudes.
- **Spring:** the typical problem is wet snow. Danger rises during the day — "early up, early down". A night without
  a hard freeze (cloudy, warm) is a warning sign.
- **Glaciers:** rope up on the way up, ski with care on the way down; snow bridges get weak in the afternoon.
- **Kit:** transceiver, shovel, probe — always. On glaciers also harness, rope, crevasse rescue kit.
