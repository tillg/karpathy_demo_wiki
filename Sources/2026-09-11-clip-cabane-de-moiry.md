# Clip: Cabane de Moiry

*Notes from a social-media clip posted 2026-09-11, rewritten in English. Figures approximate.*

- Cabane de Moiry, Swiss Alpine Club hut above Grimentz (Val d'Anniviers, Valais), right at the edge of the Moiry
  glacier.
- The draw: sunset over the glacier and a night at the hut.
- Access: toll road from Grimentz to the dam of the Lac de Moiry, then on foot to the hut.
- A comment warns that the hut is crowded in summer — book early.
