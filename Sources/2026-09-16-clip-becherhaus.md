# Clip: Becherhaus, South Tyrol

*Notes from a social-media clip posted 2026-09-16, rewritten in English. Figures approximate.*

- The Becherhaus stands at 3,195 m and is the highest mountain hut in South Tyrol, in the Stubai Alps above the
  Ridnaun valley.
- The way up is described as demanding ("definitely not easy"); the upper part is glaciated, so rope, crampons and
  experience are needed.
- Overnight stays are possible; the Müllerhütte stands opposite.
- The view is the reward for every metre of ascent.
