# Clip: Surenenpass

*Notes from a social-media clip posted 2026-08-24, rewritten in English. Figures approximate.*

*The clip is a paid promotion for the small cable cars of the region.*

- Attinghausen (Uri) to Engelberg over the Surenenpass, one of the nicest mountain hikes in central Switzerland and a
  stage of the Via Alpina.
- Small cable cars from Attinghausen up to Brüsti save about 1,000 m of ascent.
- From Brüsti through ever more alpine terrain to the pass, then down towards Engelberg. Mountain trail, not exposed.
- Tip: walk on to the Fürenalp, stop there, then take the small cable car Fürenalp–Engelberg down.
- Facts: about 14 km, about 1,100 m up, just under 800 m down, T3.
- Without the cable cars the route is a real challenge.
