# Clip: Jegihorn via ferrata, details

*Notes from a social-media clip posted 2026-09-20, rewritten in English. Figures approximate.*

- Summit 3,206 m; rated K4 by the Swiss Alpine Club.
- The ferrata itself takes about 3 hours for about 400 m of ascent, plus about 1.5 hours of approach from Kreuzboden
  or the area around the Weissmies hut.
- Highlights: a three-rope bridge over the drop; an optional variant adds another airy passage.
- About 1,000 m of steel cable, around 400 iron rungs and five ladders.
- High-alpine: experience, proper kit and good conditions only.
- Several comments from people who did it say it is less hard than the rating suggests.
