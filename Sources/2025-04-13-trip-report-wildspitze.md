# Trip report: Wildspitze, 12 April 2025 (own notes)

Two days from Vent via the Breslauer Hütte. Summit day: start 5:30, Mitterkarferner frozen hard, crampons from the
ski depot. Summit 9:45, clear views to the Similaun and the Ortler. Upper slopes (south-east) were already soft at
11:00 — next time start even earlier. Similaun looked great from up there: next on the list.
