# Notes: Alta Via 1 in the Dolomites

Collected tips for a hut-to-hut trek.

- From Lago di Braies (1,496 m) to Belluno, about 120 km, usually 8–11 days. Highest point around 2,750 m.
- Season: late June to mid-September, when the huts are open and the passes are free of snow.
- Book the huts early (they fill up in July and August).
- Not technical, but some exposed sections with cables near the end.
