# Clip: Rifugio Nuvolau

*Notes from a social-media clip posted 2026-08-24, rewritten in English. Figures approximate.*

- Rifugio Nuvolau sits at 2,575 m on the summit of Monte Nuvolau, above the Cinque Torri near Cortina d'Ampezzo.
  It is the oldest hut in the Dolomites.
- Only reachable on foot: about 1.5 hours from the Cinque Torri, 2–2.5 hours from the Passo Giau.
- Tiny and simple: 24 beds in five shared dorms, no showers, limited water and electricity.
- Spectacular views and sunsets — worth a night.
