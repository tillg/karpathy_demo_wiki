# Clip: Five Swiss mountain huts

*Notes from a social-media clip posted 2026-09-01, rewritten in English. Figures approximate.*

The point of the clip: the best way to experience the Alps is to stay at huts — ibex at dusk, quiet sunrises,
trails that empty once the day visitors leave.

- Schäfler hut, Alpstein — 1,926 m, built in 1920.
- Cabane de Moiry, Val d'Anniviers — 2,825 m, glacier right outside the window.
- Fluhalp, Zermatt — 2,606 m, Matterhorn view and reflections in the Stellisee.
- Gleckstein hut, Grindelwald — 2,317 m, ibex come by.
- Muttseehütte, Glarus — 2,501 m, next to a long dam with the view down to the Limmerensee.

Half board at all of them. For a private room in summer, book by February or March.
