# Silvretta: ski tours from the Wiesbadener Hütte (excerpt)

*Clipped from a ski-touring guide (example.org). Figures approximate.*

The Silvretta-Hochalpenstraße is closed in winter; in spring you reach the Bielerhöhe (2,037 m) by the winter
lift and bus from Partenen. The Wiesbadener Hütte (2,443 m) opens for the touring season from mid-February to early
May.

**Piz Buin (3,312 m)** — the classic. Over the Ochsentaler Gletscher to the Buinlücke, ski depot, then a short
scramble (I) to the summit. North-facing glacier, reliable snow until May.

**Dreiländerspitze (3,197 m)** — where Tyrol, Vorarlberg and Graubünden meet. From the hut over the Vermuntgletscher,
north to north-east facing, a long and gentle descent. Moderate (WS).
