# Clip: Rifugio Vicenza

*Notes from a social-media clip posted 2026-09-05, rewritten in English. Figures approximate.*

- Rifugio Vicenza sits at 2,256 m between the vertical walls of Sassolungo (Langkofel) and Sassopiatto
  (Plattkofel).
- Welcoming climbers and hikers since 1903: built by the Viennese alpine club before the First World War, later handed
  over to the Vicenza section of the CAI.
- One of the most atmospheric huts in the Dolomites, rock on all sides, views over the Alpe di Siusi.
