# Hario V60 pour-over guide (Complete Home Barista)

*Source: https://completehomebarista.com/guides/hario-v60-pour-over-guide/ (accessed 2026-10-02). Summarised in own words.*

A step-by-step brew guide for the Hario V60, one of the most popular pour-over drippers.

**Design.** The name comes from the cone's 60-degree angle. Three features define it: the conical shape, spiral ribs on
the inside wall that keep the paper off the wall so air can escape, and a single large hole at the bottom. Because
the hole lets water through fast, the brewer — not the dripper — controls the flow through grind size and pouring
speed.

**Recipe parameters:**

- Ratio 1:15 to 1:17 by weight.
- Dose around 21 g of coffee for a normal brew, about 25 g for a larger one.
- Water at roughly 90–96 °C (195–205 °F).
- Grind medium-fine, about like table salt, a little coarser than espresso.
- Bloom first: pour about 1.5 times the coffee's weight in water, wait 30–40 seconds so trapped gas escapes.
- Then pour in slow spirals; total brew time around 4 minutes, give or take 15 seconds.

**Troubleshooting:**

- Water runs through too fast and the cup tastes sour: grind finer, use hotter water or bloom longer.
- Water stalls and the cup tastes bitter: grind coarser, use slightly cooler water or shorten the brew.
- Uneven extraction: pour in spirals; make a small dimple in the bed and pour gently to avoid channels.
