# Clip: The most beautiful summit in the Stubai valley?

*Notes from a social-media clip posted 2026-09-16, rewritten in English. Figures approximate.*

- The clip asks "the most beautiful summit in the Stubai valley?" and does not name it.

- General context (not from the clip): the Wilder Freiger is a glacier summit, usually climbed in two days via the
