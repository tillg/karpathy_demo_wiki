# Clip: Alpinisteig, Sexten Dolomites

*Notes from a social-media clip posted 2026-09-13, rewritten in English. Figures approximate.*

- Alpinisteig (Strada degli Alpini): a classic via ferrata in the Sexten Dolomites below the Elferkofel, close to
  the Drei Zinnen.
- The paths were built into the rock more than 100 years ago, during the First World War — impressive and also
  sobering to walk today.
- Comments: very beautiful, but serious; usual start from the Fischleintal near Sexten, base the Zsigmondy hut.
