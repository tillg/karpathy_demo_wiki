# DAV: "Zu Gast auf Alpenvereinshütten" (staying at alpine club huts)

*Source: https://www.alpenverein.de/artikel/zu-gast-auf-alpenvereinshutten_7bf6cdc6-934f-4a00-9ff7-9829cb6180d0 (accessed 2026-10-02). Summarised in own words.*

A DAV guide for guests at alpine club huts, with the key points in brief.

**Kinds of hut.** From high mountaineering huts to family huts, huts for bikers or ski tourers, training centres and
day-trip destinations. Sleeping is mostly in dormitories or multi-bed rooms, sometimes in two-bed rooms.

**Booking.** Reserve early, especially in the summer holidays. Depending on the hut this is by online system, phone,
fax or e-mail; some huts are on the joint online reservation system of AVS, DAV, ÖAV and SAC. A deposit may be asked
for and is set against the bill. Cancel early: hut keepers may charge a cancellation fee for late cancellations.

**Members.** On category I and II huts, members pay at least €12 less per night than non-members. Members also get a
"Bergsteigeressen" (mountaineer's meal) capped at €11, with at least one vegetarian option; say in advance if you have
special dietary needs.

**Hut sleeping bag.** Compulsory for hygiene, because blankets in shared rooms are not washed after every guest.

**House rules.** No smoking inside (ashtrays outside). Everyone signs the hut book. Save water and energy — supply at
altitude is very limited and electricity expensive. Take your own rubbish back down.

**What to bring.** Towel, toiletries, a bag for rubbish, headlamp, earplugs for light sleepers, and cash, since many
huts cannot take cards.

**Other points.** Half board does not include the overnight fee; breakfast times suit early tour starts; dogs only
with the hut keeper's prior consent; usually no Wi-Fi. A hut is not a hotel.
