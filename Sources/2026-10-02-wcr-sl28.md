# SL28 — World Coffee Research variety catalogue

*Source: https://varieties.worldcoffeeresearch.org/varieties/sl28 (accessed 2026-10-02). Summarised in own words.*

SL28 is the signature variety of Kenyan coffee. It was selected in 1935 at Scott Agricultural Laboratories (the "SL"
in the name) in Kenya, from a population known as Tanganyika Drought Resistant. Genetically it belongs to the
Bourbon side of the Bourbon–Typica group. From Kenya it spread to other African countries and later to Latin
America.

**Altitude.** Suited to medium and high elevations; the catalogue's optimum depends on latitude: above about 1,200 m
within 5° of the equator, above about 900 m between 5 and 15°, above about 700 m further out.

**Cup.** Quality potential at high altitude is rated exceptional; it is one of the best known and most respected
African varieties.

**Agronomy.** Yield potential is low, though trees start bearing in their third year. It has low resistance to
coffee leaf rust and is susceptible to coffee berry disease and nematodes. Its strengths are drought tolerance and
"rusticity": trees can be left untended for long periods and come back into production. Some Kenyan SL28 trees
are 60–80 years old and still yielding.
