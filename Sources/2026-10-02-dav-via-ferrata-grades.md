# DAV / CAA: Difficulty grades of via ferratas in European comparison

*Source: https://www.alpenverein.de/files/Klettersteig-Schwierigkeitsskala_32785.pdf (accessed 2026-10-02). Summarised in own words.*

A one-page chart by the DAV and the Club Arc Alpin (CAA) that lines up the via ferrata (Klettersteig) scales used in
different Alpine countries.

- **Schall scale** (after Kurt Schall), used in Austria, South Tyrol, Trentino, Slovenia and Liechtenstein: A, A/B, B,
  B/C, C, C/D, D, D/E, E, E/F.
- **Hüsler scale** (after Eugen Hüsler), used in Germany, Switzerland and Liechtenstein: K1 easy, K2 medium, K3 fairly
  difficult, K4 difficult, K5 very difficult, K6 extremely difficult.
- **Italy:** F, PD, D, MD, ED (from "facile" to "estrema difficoltà").
- **France:** F, PD, AD, D, TD, ED (from "facile" to "extrêmement difficile").
- Italy and France may use intermediate steps such as F+ or PD−.

A **colour scale** links the systems: blue = easy, blue/red = moderately difficult, red = difficult, red/black = very
difficult, black = extremely difficult. Roughly, A–A/B and K1 sit at the easy end, and E–E/F and K6 at the extreme end.

The chart only aligns the scales; for the exact wording of each grade it refers readers to guidebooks, textbooks and
online publications.
