# Trip report: Piz Buin, 20 April 2024 (own notes)

Up to the Wiesbadener Hütte on the 19th. Left at 6:00, cold clear night, hard crust. Ochsentaler Gletscher in perfect
condition, ski depot at the Buinlücke at 9:30, summit 10:15, very windy. Descent on corn from 11:00, perfect. Back at
the Bielerhöhe 14:00. Would do the Dreiländerspitze next time — we saw it from the summit.
