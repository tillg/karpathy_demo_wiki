# Clip: Fellaria glacier

*Notes from a social-media clip posted 2026-08-06, rewritten in English. Figures approximate.*

- The Fellaria glacier in Valmalenco (Italian Alps) is locally called the "mini Perito Moreno". No road leads
  there, which keeps it quiet.
- Access: a landslide had closed the road from Lanzada to Campo Moro for a whole summer. Park at Tornadri (1,100 m),
  walk 30–40 minutes through the forest (about 200 m up) to a free shuttle bus. Check the timetable — missing it means
  750 m of extra ascent.
- Hike in: from the shuttle along the Campo Moro dam, then the Gera dam and the lake, then up to the Rifugio Bignami —
  about 2 hours and 431 m.
- The hut (2,400 m) was built in 1955 in memory of a climber who died on a Himalayan expedition the year before. It
  faces the glacier; excellent dinners.
- From the hut another 360 m, about 2.5 hours one way, to the lake at the glacier's edge.
- Possible in one (hard) day; better split with a night at the hut.
