# DAV: Structure and organs of the Deutscher Alpenverein

*Source: https://www.alpenverein.de/verband/ueber-den-dav/struktur-des-dav (accessed 2026-10-02). Summarised in own words.*

The DAV's own page on how the association is organised.

**What it is.** The Deutscher Alpenverein e.V. is a non-profit association for mountain sports and nature protection.
It is a federation of more than 350 legally independent clubs called sections, which together form a "solidarity
community". With about 1.6 million members (2025) it calls itself the largest mountain sports association in the world
and the third-largest sports federation in Germany.

**Organs.**

- *Hauptversammlung* (general assembly) — the highest body, meeting once a year.
- *Verbandsrat* and *Präsidium* (federal council and presidium) — the governing bodies.
- *Bundesgeschäftsstelle* (federal office) — runs the day-to-day work and serves the sections.

There are also presidential committees, commissions, project groups and regional bodies (section meetings and state
associations).

**Related bodies.** The youth organisation JDAV is an independent youth-welfare body; the DAV Summit Club GmbH, a
wholly owned subsidiary, is the mountaineering school. Office holders work on a voluntary basis.

**Huts and trails.** The association, through its sections, looks after huts and mountain paths across the Alps.
