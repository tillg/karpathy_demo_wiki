# Cevedale from Rifugio Pizzini

*Clipped from a touring blog (example.org).*

The Cevedale (3,769 m) in the Ortler Alps is one of the great spring ski tours of the Italian Alps. From the Forni
car park (2,178 m) above Santa Caterina Valfurva it's an easy hour up to the Rifugio Pizzini (2,706 m), which opens
for the spring season in March. Next morning over the Vedretta del Cevedale (west to north-west facing) to the
Casati col and on to the summit. Huge, open glacier slopes — best from April, when days are long and the snow has
settled. Moderate (WS).
