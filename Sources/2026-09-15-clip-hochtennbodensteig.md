# Clip: Hochtennbodensteig

*Notes from a social-media clip posted 2026-09-15, rewritten in English. Figures approximate.*

- Hochtennbodensteig, Axamer Lizum (south-west of Innsbruck).
- Start at the Hoadl, up to the Hochtennboden and below the Kalkkögel all the way to the Birgitzköpflhaus; take the
  lift down from there.
- Facts: 2.5 hours, 6.4 km, 400 m of ascent.
