# SCA water standards (Coffee Parts glossary)

*Source: https://know.coffeeparts.com.au/glossary/sca-water-standards (accessed 2026-10-02). Summarised in own words.*

A short glossary entry from an Australian café-equipment supplier on the Specialty Coffee Association's guidelines for
brewing water. Its starting point is simple: great coffee depends on appropriate water.

**Key values quoted from the SCA standard:**

- *Total dissolved solids (TDS) of the water:* about 75–150 ppm.
- *Calcium hardness:* about 50–80 mg/L (expressed as CaCO₃).
- *pH:* about 6.5–7.5, i.e. close to neutral.

The entry notes that the full standard also covers chlorine, odour, total alkalinity and sodium, without giving the
numbers for those.

**Practical consequence.** Many cities have water far harder than this range. Cafés in such hard-water areas (the entry
names most of Sydney, all of Adelaide and parts of Perth) need filtration to get into the SCA window.

Cross-check: the 75–150 ppm range for the water's dissolved solids is the same figure the Seattle Coffee Gear Gold Cup
guide gives, so the two sources agree on that point.
