# Coffee

From cherry to cup: where coffee grows, which plants it comes from, how it is processed and roasted, and how to brew it
at home.

**Basics.** About 70 countries grow coffee. Two species: Arabica (~60 % of output, cooler highlands, 0.8–1.4 % caffeine)
and Robusta (~40 %, warmer lowlands, 1.7–4.0 % caffeine). Latin America grows mostly Arabica, Asia mostly Robusta.

**Origins:** [[coffee-ethiopia]] (where Arabica comes from) · [[coffee-kenya]] (washed, SL varieties) ·
[[coffee-brazil]] (a third of world coffee, naturals).

**Varieties:** [[typica]] and [[bourbon]] (the two founding lines) · [[sl28]] (Kenya) · [[geisha]] (Ethiopian landrace,
Panama fame).

**From cherry to bean:** [[coffee-processing]] (washed, honey, natural) → [[roast-levels]] (light to dark).
