# Topology

The branch of [[mathematics]] about properties that survive continuous deformation — stretching and bending, but
no tearing or gluing. Branches: general (point-set), algebraic, differential and geometric topology.

## Timeline

- 1736 — [[leonhard-euler]] and the Königsberg bridges; later his polyhedron formula ([[euler-characteristic]]).
- 1847 — Listing coins "topology".
- 1854 — [[bernhard-riemann]]'s lecture introducing manifolds.
- 1895 — [[henri-poincare]]'s *Analysis Situs*: [[homotopy]], [[fundamental-group]], homology.
- 1909–1912 — [[luitzen-brouwer]]: fixed-point theorem, invariance of dimension.
- 1914 — [[felix-hausdorff]] defines topological spaces.
- c. 1925 — [[emmy-noether]]: homology *groups* instead of Betti numbers.
- 2002–2003 — [[grigori-perelman]] proves the [[poincare-conjecture]].
