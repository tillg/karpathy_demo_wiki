# Source: Fundamental group (Wikipedia)

Loops up to [[homotopy]] form a group; trivial for the plane and sphere, ℤ for the circle, ℤ × ℤ for the torus. Basis of [[fundamental-group]]; needed for the [[poincare-conjecture]].
