# Source: Coffee production (Wikipedia)

Where coffee grows: ~70 countries, Latin America mostly Arabica, Asia mostly Robusta; Brazil and Vietnam lead; Arabica just under 60 % of output. Basis for [[coffee]] and the origin pages [[coffee-brazil]], [[coffee-ethiopia]].
