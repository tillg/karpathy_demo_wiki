# Similaun vs Cevedale for late April

Two glacier ski tours, both WS, both at their best in spring — and neither done yet. Late April
sits inside both seasons: above ~2,500 m on glaciers the snow usually holds until mid-May
([[spring-snow]]), and both ascents face north-west-ish, the aspect that keeps snow longest
([[aspect]]). The differences are logistics, altitude, and how well the vault knows each area.

## What late April means for both

- **Snow:** late April is comfortably mid-season for Similaun (its NW glacier "holds good snow
  well into May") and the core window for Cevedale ("best from April, when days are long and the
  snow has settled"). Neither needs cold mid-winter conditions; both want the spring routine:
  early start, [[corn-snow]] late morning, off the glacier before the wet afternoon snow
  ([[spring-snow]]).
- **Glacier kit:** both cross glaciers — rope, harness, crevasse rescue kit and a roped party are
  mandatory; snow bridges weaken through the day, which is another reason the summit day starts
  early ([[glacier-travel]]).
- **Huts:** both open for the spring season in March, so in a normal year both are serviced in
  late April — but the vault can't confirm actual opening dates for a specific year.

## Arguments for Similaun

Known ground: the Vent approach and the Ötztal huts are familiar from [[wildspitze]] (done
12 April 2025, see [[tour-log]]), and Similaun "looked like the obvious next one" from that
summit. Slightly lower and a bit less summit-day climbing; the NW aspect means there is no
rush — it still works if late April turns out warm, and it pairs naturally with
[[fluchtkogel]] from the [[vernagt-hut]] for a longer hut week.

## Arguments for Cevedale

The easier shape for a two-day trip: the hut is an easy hour (~530 m) from the car park, so day
1 is short and the summit day is a normal ~1,060 m glacier morning. Higher summit (3,769 m),
"huge, open glacier slopes", and new terrain — the vault has nothing done in the Ortler Alps,
only one blog source. Late April is exactly when the tour is at its best.

## Verdict (synthesis, not a source claim)

For a single late-April weekend, **Cevedale** fits better: shorter approach, prime season, and
the higher summit. Pick **Similaun** instead to build on the Ötztal experience, if you prefer
familiar ground, or if the trip can extend beyond a weekend — the vault has wanted it since the
Wildspitze day.

## What this vault doesn't know

- Today's snowpack, crevasse/bridge state and avalanche danger in either range — check the
  official avalanche bulletins and hut pages before committing.
- Actual hut opening dates and bookings for a given late-April week.
- Any time estimate for the Niedertal approach to the Martin-Busch-Hütte.
- Firsthand impressions of either tour: both `done: null`; Cevedale is known only from one blog
  post, Similaun only from the guidebook and the view from Wildspitze.
