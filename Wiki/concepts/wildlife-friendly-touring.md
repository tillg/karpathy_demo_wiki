# Wildlife-friendly touring

The [[deutscher-alpenverein]] campaign
**"Natürlich auf Tour"** (since 1995 as "Skibergsteigen umweltfreundlich", with the Bavarian environment authorities)
covers the Bavarian Alps (the vault's [[bavarian-prealps]] tours [[hirschberg]] and [[brecherspitz]] lie in this area; the page doesn't name them):

- about 500 recommended ski routes and variants on some 180 summits;
- 225 forest and wildlife protection zones to avoid completely — key habitat of capercaillie and black grouse;
- shown on the DAV "BY" maps, signs on site, labelled guidebooks, alpenvereinaktiv.com and OpenStreetMap;
- voluntary, no fines — but the DAV warns bans could follow if zones are ignored.

General rules from the DAV ski-touring recommendations: mind wildlife, respect protected areas, keep off young forest
plantings, share cars or take public transport. Check the zones when planning ([[tour-planning-3x3]]).
