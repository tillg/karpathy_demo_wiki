# Manifold

**Intuition:** a space that looks flat if you zoom in far enough — like the Earth to someone standing on it.

**Definition:** an n-dimensional manifold is a [[topological-space]] in which every point has a neighbourhood
[[homeomorphism|homeomorphic]] to an open piece of n-dimensional Euclidean space. A *chart* is such a local map; an
*atlas* is a set of charts covering the whole space.

**Examples:** lines and circles (dimension 1); spheres, tori, the Klein bottle (dimension 2 — *surfaces*). Not a
manifold: the figure-eight, because its crossing point doesn't look like a line.

Idea from [[bernhard-riemann]] (1854). Surfaces are fully listed by the [[classification-of-surfaces]]; 3-manifolds
are where the [[poincare-conjecture]] lives.
