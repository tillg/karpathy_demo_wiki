# Avalanche danger scale

Five levels, 1 (low) to 5 (very high); most accidents happen at 2 and 3. The bulletin also names the avalanche problem (new snow, wind slab, persistent weak layer, wet snow, gliding snow) and the aspects and altitudes it applies to. In spring the typical problem is wet snow, rising during the day. Always check the official bulletin before a tour.
