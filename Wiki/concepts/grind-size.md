# Grind size

Finer grind slows the flow and extends contact time, so more [[extraction]]; coarser does the opposite. Match it to the method:

| Method | Grind | Contact time |
|---|---|---|
| [[espresso-machine]] | fine | seconds, under pressure |
| [[v60]] | medium-fine (table salt) | ~4 min |
| drip / pour-over default | medium | 3–4 min (pour-over), 4–8 min (drip machine) |
| [[french-press]] | coarse | ~4 min |

Rule of thumb: weak or sour → finer; harsh or bitter → coarser. Change one variable at a time.
