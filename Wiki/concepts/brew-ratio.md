# Brew ratio

Coffee to water, by weight — weigh both, don't use scoops.

- **Gold Cup default:** 55 g per litre ≈ 1:18 for filter coffee ([[chemex]], [[french-press]]).
- **National norms vary:** from ~55 g/L (US) to ~63 g/L (Norway).
- **[[v60]] recipe:** 1:15–1:17, i.e. a bit stronger than the Gold Cup default.

The ratio sets strength (TDS); taste problems at the right strength are an [[extraction]] issue — fix them with
[[grind-size]] rather than more coffee.
