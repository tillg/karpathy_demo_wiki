# Via ferrata grades

Several scales exist side by side; a DAV / Club Arc Alpin chart aligns them:

- **Schall** (Austria, South Tyrol, Trentino, Slovenia, Liechtenstein): A, A/B, B, B/C, C, C/D, D, D/E, E, E/F.
- **Hüsler** (Germany, Switzerland, Liechtenstein): K1 easy … K6 extremely difficult.
- **Italy:** F, PD, D, MD, ED. **France:** F, PD, AD, D, TD, ED.
- **Colour scale:** blue easy → blue/red → red difficult → red/black → black extremely difficult.

The chart only aligns the scales; exact grade descriptions are in guidebooks. Trails without fixed protection use
[[mountain-trail-grades]].
