# Topological space

**Intuition:** a set of points plus just enough structure to say what "nearby" means — no distances, no angles.

**Definition:** a set X with a collection of subsets, the [[open-set]]s, such that the empty set and X are open,
any union of open sets is open, and any finite intersection of open sets is open.

**Examples:**
- the real line with the usual open intervals (every metric space works the same way, with open balls);
- the *discrete* topology (every subset open) and the *trivial* one (only the empty set and X open) — the two
  extremes on any set.

First defined by [[felix-hausdorff]] in 1914. Everything else — [[continuity]], [[homeomorphism]],
[[connectedness]], [[compactness]] — is phrased in terms of open sets. Part of [[topology]].
