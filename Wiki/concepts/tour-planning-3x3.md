# Tour planning (3x3)

A planning grid promoted by the [[deutscher-alpenverein]]: three factors — **conditions** (weather, snow), **terrain**
(route, steepness, [[aspect]], length) and **people** (fitness, experience, group) — checked in three phases, giving
nine fields.

The two DAV pages name the phases slightly differently: the avalanche article uses *planning → on site → the single
slope*; the general planning explainer uses *planning → checkpoints on tour → unexpected events*. Same idea: re-check
whenever new information arrives.

Planning checklist from the DAV pages: several mountain forecasts (about 0.6 °C colder per 100 m), the avalanche
bulletin ([[avalanche-danger-scale]]), steep or exposed passages, a realistic time budget (≈15 min rest every 2 h,
30 min on top), checkpoints with target times, alternatives decided in advance, plan for the weakest member, tell
someone your route. A "risk box" (each factor green or red) is one way to decide go / no-go.

In spring, timing is part of the conditions factor: see [[spring-snow]] and [[corn-snow]]. For the slope phase in
winter see [[avalanche-decision-making]].
