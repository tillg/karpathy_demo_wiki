# Spring snow

Where the snow lasts in spring, as rules of thumb for an average winter in the Eastern Alps:

| Altitude | Late March | April | May |
|---|---|---|---|
| below ~1,500 m | usually gone or patchy | rarely enough | no |
| 1,500–2,200 m | north faces good, south faces melting out | north faces often still good | rarely |
| above ~2,500 m and glaciers | good | good | usually good until mid-May |

North faces keep snow longest, south and east faces melt out first ([[aspect]]). The daily freeze–thaw cycle gives
[[corn-snow]] in the late morning and wet, avalanche-prone snow in the afternoon: start early.

Every winter differs — this vault can't know today's snow depth or the avalanche bulletin.
