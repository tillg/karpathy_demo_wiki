# Water for coffee

Brewing water is an ingredient. SCA targets, as quoted by the vault's sources:

| Parameter | Target |
|---|---|
| Dissolved solids (TDS) | 75–150 ppm |
| Calcium hardness | 50–80 mg/L as CaCO₃ |
| pH | 6.5–7.5 |
| Temperature at the grounds | 90–96 °C |

The full SCA standard also covers chlorine, odour, alkalinity and sodium (no numbers in the sources). Hard tap water
needs filtering to reach these values; for home brewing the Gold Cup guide simply recommends filtered water. Wrong water skews [[extraction]] even with the right [[brew-ratio]].
