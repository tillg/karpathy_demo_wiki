# Extraction

How much of the ground coffee ends up dissolved in the cup. The Gold Cup Standard targets two numbers at once:

| Measure | What it is | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Extraction yield | share of the grounds' mass dissolved | 18–22 % |
| Strength (TDS) | dissolved coffee in the cup | 1.15–1.35 % |

Strength is set mainly by the [[brew-ratio]]; extraction by [[grind-size]], water temperature (90–96 °C) and contact
time. Too little → thin and sour; too much → harsh and bitter. Keep ratio and temperature fixed and tune with the
grind — e.g. the [[v60]] troubleshooting rules. Leaving a [[french-press]] standing on its grounds over-extracts.
Water chemistry matters too: [[water-for-coffee]].
