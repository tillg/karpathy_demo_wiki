# Hut etiquette (alpine club huts)

What the DAV asks of guests on alpine club huts (huts in this vault: [[wiesbadener-hut]], [[martin-busch-hut]],
[[vernagt-hut]]; the Italian [[rifugio-pizzini]] may differ):

- **Book early**, especially in summer; online (joint AVS/DAV/ÖAV/SAC system for some huts), phone or e-mail. A
  deposit may be due; cancel early, late cancellations can be charged.
- **Members** pay at least €12 less per night on category I and II huts; the "mountaineer's meal" is capped at €11 with
  a vegetarian option.
- **Hut sleeping bag** is compulsory (hygiene). Sign the hut book. No smoking inside.
- Save water and energy, take your rubbish down, bring cash, headlamp, towel, earplugs. Dogs only by arrangement; no
  Wi-Fi. A hut is not a hotel.
