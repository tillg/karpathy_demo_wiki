# Mountain trail grades (signposts and SAC T1–T6)

Signposts in the Alps colour-code the difficulty: **blue** = easy, **red** = medium, **black** = difficult (waymarks on
rocks are red-white-red regardless). The DAV maps these to the Swiss Alpine Club hiking scale:

| Sign | SAC | What it means (DAV summary) |
|---|---|---|
| — | T1 | simple valley path |
| blue | ~T2 | narrow, can be steep, no fall-risk sections |
| red | ~T3 | steep, exposed bits, short cable or chain sections |
| black | T4–T6 | hands needed, simple climbing; T5–T6 often pathless, up to climbing grade II, ice axe and rope skills |

Grades assume good conditions — wet rock or old snow make a route much harder. Secured routes have their own scale:
[[via-ferrata-grades]]. Summer hikes in this vault: [[zugspitze-reintal]], [[alta-via-1]].
