# Alpine emergency

From the DAV pages (numbers as listed there in 2026 — check before a trip):

- **112** works across the EU, gets network priority and works on a locked phone; without own-network reception, restart
  the phone and dial 112 instead of the PIN. Outside Germany and South Tyrol it may reach a general centre first.
- Direct mountain rescue: **140** Austria, **1414** Switzerland (Rega), 144 Valais, 117/144 Liechtenstein; Germany,
  South Tyrol/Italy, France and Slovenia: 112.
- **Prepare:** charged phone kept warm, own number written down waterproof, know your position (map, altimeter).
  Apps: SOS-EU-ALP (Bavaria, Austria, South Tyrol), Rega app (Switzerland).
- **On the call:** stay calm, answer every question (where, what, how many injured, weather), hang up only when the
  dispatcher does, keep the line free.
- **No reception:** two people look for reception, the rest give first aid. **Alpine distress signal:** 6 signals a
  minute, 1 minute pause, repeat; answer is 3 a minute.

Phone and first-aid kit are part of the [[avalanche-safety-kit]].
