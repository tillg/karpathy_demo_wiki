# Grigori Perelman (born 1966)

Russian mathematician, born in Leningrad (St Petersburg).

- Proved the [[poincare-conjecture]] in three arXiv papers (November 2002 – July 2003), building on Richard
  Hamilton's Ricci flow.
- Declined the Fields Medal (2006) and the Clay Millennium Prize (2010), saying Hamilton deserved as much credit.
