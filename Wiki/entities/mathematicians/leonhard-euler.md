# Leonhard Euler (1707–1783)

Swiss mathematician, born in Basel, died in St Petersburg. Counted as the first to think "topologically":

- **Königsberg bridges** (presented 1735, published 1741): no walk crosses all seven bridges exactly once, because
  only the *number* of bridges at each land mass matters — the start of graph theory and of [[topology]].
- **Polyhedron formula** V − E + F = 2, today the [[euler-characteristic]].

Dates differ between sources: the Wikipedia topology article puts the formula at 1750, the Euler characteristic
article at 1758, MacTutor at "1736–1752".
