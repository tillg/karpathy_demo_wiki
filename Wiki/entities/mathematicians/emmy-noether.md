# Emmy Noether (1882–1935)

German mathematician, born in Erlangen, died in Bryn Mawr (USA). Founder of modern abstract algebra.

Her mark on [[topology]]: around 1925 she pointed out that the numbers topologists counted (Betti numbers, torsion
coefficients) are shadows of *groups* — [[homology]] groups. That move from numbers to algebra is why algebraic
topology looks the way it does; the [[euler-characteristic]] is now computed from these groups. Dismissed by the
Nazis in 1933.
