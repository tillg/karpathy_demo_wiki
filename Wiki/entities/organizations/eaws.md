# European Avalanche Warning Services (EAWS)

Umbrella of the European avalanche warning services (avalanches.org). It supports its members in preventing deaths and
damage from avalanches and publishes the common five-level [[avalanche-danger-scale]], the avalanche problems, a
glossary, forecasting standards and fatality statistics. Per EAWS, level 3 (considerable) accounts for about half of
avalanche deaths.

For today's danger, read the regional bulletin of the warning service for your area.
