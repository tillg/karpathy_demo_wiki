# Deutscher Alpenverein (DAV)

German Alpine Club (alpenverein.de): a non-profit association for mountain sports and nature protection, a federation
of 350+ independent **sections** with about 1.6 million members (2025) — by its own account the world's largest
mountain sports association. Governed by the annual general assembly, the federal council and presidium; the federal
office runs daily work. Youth: JDAV. Mountaineering school: DAV Summit Club. Sections look after huts and trails across
the Alps.

What the vault takes from it: [[tour-planning-3x3]], [[avalanche-safety-kit]], [[avalanche-decision-making]],
[[wildlife-friendly-touring]] ("Natürlich auf Tour"), [[hut-etiquette]], [[alpine-emergency]],
[[mountain-trail-grades]], [[via-ferrata-grades]]. Its 10 ski-touring recommendations were agreed with the other
Alpine clubs (incl. ÖAV, SAC, CAI, AVS).
