# Bourbon

One of the two founding Arabica lines (with [[typica]]). French missionaries took it from Yemen to Bourbon island (La
Réunion) in the early 1700s; it reached [[coffee-brazil]] around 1860 and spread across Latin America. [[sl28]] is a
Bourbon-related descendant.

- **Cup:** very good to excellent at high altitude.
- **Farming:** medium yield; susceptible to leaf rust, coffee berry disease and nematodes — often replaced by resistant cultivars.
