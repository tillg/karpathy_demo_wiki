# Geisha (Gesha)

Collected from Ethiopian coffee forests in the 1930s ([[coffee-ethiopia]]), via Tanzania to Central America (1953). Became
the star of specialty coffee in 2005, when a Panamanian lot broke auction records at over 20 USD a pound.

- **Cup:** delicate and perfumed — floral, jasmine, peach. Only at high altitude.
- **Farming:** low yield, intermediate rust resistance, susceptible to coffee berry disease and nematodes — hence the price.
- **Brewing:** a light roast on a clean pour-over ([[v60]], [[chemex]]) keeps the florals; see [[which-brewer-for-which-bean]].
