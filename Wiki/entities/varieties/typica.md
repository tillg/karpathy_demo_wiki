# Typica

The other founding Arabica line next to [[bourbon]]. From [[coffee-ethiopia]] via India's Malabar coast to Java (seeds
sent 1696 and 1699), then to the Americas, where it dominated until about the 1940s.

- **Cup:** good, very good potential at altitude.
- **Farming:** tall, low-yielding (planted at 3,000–4,000 plants/ha to compensate), highly susceptible to leaf rust.
