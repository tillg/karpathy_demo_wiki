# SL28

Kenya's signature variety, selected in 1935 at Scott Agricultural Laboratories from "Tanganyika Drought Resistant"
coffee. On the [[bourbon]] side of the Bourbon–Typica group.

- **Cup:** exceptional at altitude; one of Africa's most respected varieties.
- **Farming:** drought tolerant and "rustic" — trees 60–80 years old still bear. But low yield and weak against leaf
  rust, coffee berry disease and nematodes, which is why [[coffee-kenya]] also plants Batian and Ruiru 11.
