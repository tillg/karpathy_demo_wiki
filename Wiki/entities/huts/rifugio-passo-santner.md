# Rifugio Passo Santner (approx. 2,734 m)

Modern, much-debated hut on an edge of the Rosengarten (Catinaccio), [[dolomites]], with the view straight from the dining room. Up via the Passo Santner via ferrata (not a hard one) or from the Fassa valley past the Preuss, Vajolet and Re Alberto huts. No tour page yet.
