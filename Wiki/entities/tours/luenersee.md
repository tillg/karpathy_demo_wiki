# Lünersee (approx. 1,970 m)

Turquoise reservoir in the [[raetikon]] (Vorarlberg) with the [[douglass-hut]] right on the shore. Up from Brand with the Lünerseebahn or on foot; easy walk round the lake. Reachable entirely by public transport.

- **Plan:** night in the hut's dormitory; next day on to the Totalphütte, or higher up for the view.
