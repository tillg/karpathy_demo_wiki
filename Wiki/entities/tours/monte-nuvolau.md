# Monte Nuvolau (2,575 m)

Short hike to the summit of Monte Nuvolau above the Cinque Torri near Cortina d'Ampezzo, crowned by the [[rifugio-nuvolau]], the oldest hut in the [[dolomites]]. About 1.5 h from the Cinque Torri (approx. 2,250 m), 2–2.5 h from the Passo Giau; only reachable on foot.

- **Plan:** stay the night for sunset — the hut has only 24 beds.
