# Jegihorn via ferrata (3,206 m)

Above Saas-Grund, called the highest via ferrata in Switzerland. Lift to Kreuzboden (approx. 2,400 m), about 1.5 h of approach towards the [[weissmies-hut]], then about 3 h and 400 m on the ferrata: three-rope bridge, exposed arête, five ladders, around 1,000 m of steel cable.

- **Grade:** K4 by the SAC ([[via-ferrata-grades]]); several people who did it found it easier than that. High-alpine — stable weather only.
