# Becher / Becherhaus (3,195 m)

From Maiern at the head of the Ridnaun valley in South Tyrol (approx. 1,420 m) up to the [[becherhaus]] on the Becher, the highest hut in South Tyrol. Long high-alpine ascent of approx. 1,800 m; the upper part is glaciated — rope and crampons ([[glacier-travel]]).

- **Plan:** two days with a night at the hut; the Müllerhütte stands opposite.
