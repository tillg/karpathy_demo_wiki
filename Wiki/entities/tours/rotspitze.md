# Rotspitze (approx. 2,067 m)

Ridge loop in the [[rofan]] above the Achensee. Cable car up to the [[erfurter-hut]] (approx. 1,830 m), then about 4 h, 8 km and 550 m on a black (difficult) trail with two short cabled sections. The Achensee below, the [[karwendel]] in front.

- **Note:** easy if you are fine with heights — though one comment calls it a hard hike with scrambling.
- **Not done yet.** Good day trip from Munich or Innsbruck.
