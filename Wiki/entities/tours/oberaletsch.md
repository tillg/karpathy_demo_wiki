# Oberaletsch hut (approx. 2,640 m)

4–5 hours into the remote Oberaletsch valley to the [[oberaletsch-hut]], surrounded by glaciers. Through alpine meadows, across a glacial river and along a panorama trail high above the shrinking Oberaletsch glacier; the last kilometres keep going up and down. Usually from the Belalp lift above Blatten (approx. 2,090 m).

- **Beyond the hut:** no more trails, only glaciers and cliffs for mountaineers.
