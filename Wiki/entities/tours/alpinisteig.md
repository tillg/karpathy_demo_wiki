# Alpinisteig / Strada degli Alpini (approx. 2,700 m)

Classic via ferrata in the Sexten [[dolomites]], on ledges below the Elferkofel, not far from the Drei Zinnen. The paths were cut into the rock during the First World War. Start in the Fischleintal near Sexten (approx. 1,450 m), base the [[zsigmondy-hut]]; a long day with approx. 1,000 m+ of ascent.

- **Needs:** via-ferrata experience, kit and good weather.
