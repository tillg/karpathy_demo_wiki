# Mittenwalder Höhenweg (approx. 2,370 m)

Ridge traverse above Mittenwald in the [[karwendel]]: up with the Karwendelbahn (top station approx. 2,244 m), along the fully cabled ridge, down via the [[brunnstein-hut]] to the valley. 6–7 h in total, approx. 1,300 m of descent.

- **Grade:** A/B, easy to moderate ([[via-ferrata-grades]]) — but via-ferrata kit, sure feet and a head for heights are needed.
- **Season:** July to October; old snow on the approach and descent in early summer.
- **Not done yet.**
