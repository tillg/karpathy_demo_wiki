# Latemar: Rifugio Torre di Pisa (2,671 m)

Up into the Latemar group to the [[rifugio-torre-di-pisa]], named after a leaning rock tower. From Obereggen a chairlift shortens the way to the Passo Feudo (approx. 2,200 m); approx. 2.5 h to the hut. Also a stage of the Latemar high route.

- **Why:** stay overnight — at sunrise the towers glow orange for a few minutes.
- **Not done yet.** Check the exact route before going.
