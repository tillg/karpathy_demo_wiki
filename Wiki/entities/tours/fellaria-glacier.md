# Fellaria glacier (approx. 2,760 m)

Hike to the lake at the edge of the Fellaria glacier in [[valmalenco]], the "mini Perito Moreno". From Tornadri (1,100 m) 30–40 min to a free shuttle bus (missing it adds 750 m), then along the Campo Moro and Gera dams to the [[rifugio-bignami]] (2,400 m; approx. 2 h, 431 m), then approx. 2.5 h and 360 m more to the glacier lake.

- **Plan:** one long day, or better two with a night at the hut.
- **Access:** a landslide closed the road from Lanzada to Campo Moro for a summer — check before going.
