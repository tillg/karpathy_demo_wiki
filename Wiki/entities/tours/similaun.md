# Similaun (3,599 m)

From Vent through the Niedertal to the [[martin-busch-hut]], then over the Niederjochferner. North-west facing glacier that holds good snow well into May. Moderate.

- **Snow:** one of the most reliable spring tours in the [[oetztal-alps]] (altitude + north-west aspect, see [[aspect]]).
