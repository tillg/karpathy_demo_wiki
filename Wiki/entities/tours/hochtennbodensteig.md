# Hochtennbodensteig (2,340 m)

Panorama path in the Axamer Lizum south-west of Innsbruck, right below the Kalkkögel ([[stubai-alps]]). Lift up to the Hoadl (2,340 m), over the Hochtennboden to the [[birgitzkoepflhaus]] (1,808 m), lift back down. 2.5 h, 6.4 km, 400 m of ascent — mostly downhill, family-friendly.

- **Season:** tied to the summer operating times of the lifts.
