# Hirschberg (1,670 m)

Short classic above Kreuth near the Tegernsee: 900 m of ascent through the forest past the Hirschberghaus.

- **Snow:** low altitude — only in mid-winter; usually no snow in the forest from March ([[spring-snow]]).
