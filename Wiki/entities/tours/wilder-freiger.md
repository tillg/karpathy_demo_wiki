# Wilder Freiger (3,418 m)

Glacier summit at the head of the Stubai valley, usually two days with a night at the [[sulzenau-hut]] or the [[nuernberger-hut]]. Approx. 1,900 m of ascent from Grawa (approx. 1,530 m); rope and crampons ([[glacier-travel]]).
