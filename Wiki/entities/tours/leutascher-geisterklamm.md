# Leutascher Geisterklamm (approx. 1,100 m)

Gorge on the Bavarian–Tyrolean border between Mittenwald and Leutasch ([[wetterstein]]). Its oldest part, the Wasserfallsteig, is a narrow wooden walkway (about 200 m) just above the water, ending at a 23 m waterfall. Approx. 150 m of ascent, about 2 h; sturdy shoes and a head for heights.

- **Season:** May to mid-October, closed in winter. Entrance fee, cash only; no dogs, prams or bikes on the walkways.
- **Not done yet.** Family-friendly.
