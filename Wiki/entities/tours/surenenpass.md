# Surenenpass (approx. 2,291 m)

Pass crossing from Attinghausen (Uri) to Engelberg, a stage of the Via Alpina ([[uri-alps]]). Small cable cars up to Brüsti (approx. 1,525 m, saves approx. 1,000 m) and down from the Fürenalp to Engelberg. About 14 km, approx. 1,100 m up and 800 m down; mountain trail, not exposed (T3, [[mountain-trail-grades]]).

- **Tip:** stop at the Fürenalp instead of descending straight to the valley.
- **Not done yet.** Without the lifts it is a much harder day.
