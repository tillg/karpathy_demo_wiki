# Chemex

Peter Schlumbohm's 1941 hourglass of glass with a wooden collar and leather tie — dripper and carafe in one, and in
MoMA's collection.

- **Character:** the thick bonded filter holds back most coffee oils → a very clean, light-bodied cup (and less
  cafestol).
- **Recipe:** the source gives none; the vault uses the Gold Cup defaults for pour-over — about 55 g/L
  ([[brew-ratio]]), 90–96 °C, 3–4 minutes ([[extraction]]).
- Versus [[v60]]: cleaner, less body. See [[which-brewer-for-which-bean]].
