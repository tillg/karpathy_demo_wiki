# Poincaré conjecture

**Statement:** every closed, simply connected 3-[[manifold]] (trivial [[fundamental-group]]) is homeomorphic to the
3-sphere.

- Posed by [[henri-poincare]] in 1904. The 2-dimensional version follows from the [[classification-of-surfaces]].
- Higher dimensions came first: Smale (n ≥ 5, 1961), Freedman (n = 4, 1982).
- [[grigori-perelman]] proved dimension 3 (arXiv, 2002–2003) using Hamilton's Ricci flow "with surgery".
- The only solved Millennium Prize Problem; Perelman declined the prize (2010) and the Fields Medal (2006).
