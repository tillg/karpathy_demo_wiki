# Brouwer fixed-point theorem

**Statement:** a [[continuity|continuous]] map f from a nonempty compact convex set to itself has a point with
f(x) = x.

- **Pictures:** crumple a sheet of paper and put it back on its twin — some point lies straight above its original
  spot; a map of a country spread on the floor of that country has a true "you are here" point.
- **Every assumption matters:** x ↦ x + 1 on the real line has no fixed point (not [[compactness|compact]]); rotating
  a circle by 180° has none (the circle has a hole, the disk doesn't).
- **In dimension 1** it is the intermediate value theorem.
- **History:** Bohl (3D, 1904), [[luitzen-brouwer]] (1909–1910), Hadamard (1910).
