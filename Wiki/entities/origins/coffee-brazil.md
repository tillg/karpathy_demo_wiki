# Brazil

The world's largest producer for about 150 years, with roughly a third of all coffee. First planted in Pará in 1727.

- **Where:** ~27,000 km² of plantations, mostly in the south-east; Minas Gerais alone grows about half. Arabica
  centres on Minas Gerais, Robusta on Espírito Santo.
- **Harvest:** dry season, June–September.
- **Processing:** almost entirely dry / natural ([[coffee-processing]]) — whole cherries sun-dried 8–10 days, then hulled.
  About 90 % of Brazil's Arabica is dry-processed.
- **Varieties:** [[bourbon]] arrived around 1860 and spread from here through South and Central America.
- **Cup:** the sources give no tasting notes; natural processing is associated with a sweeter, fruitier cup
  ([[coffee-processing]]). For brewer choice see [[which-brewer-for-which-bean]].
