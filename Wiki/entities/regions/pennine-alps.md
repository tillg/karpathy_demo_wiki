# Pennine Alps (Valais)

Range south of the Rhône valley in Valais with most of the Swiss 4,000 m peaks; side valleys such as the Saas valley and the Val d'Anniviers.

Tours: [[jegihorn]], [[lac-de-moiry]]. Huts: [[cabane-de-moiry]], [[weissmies-hut]].
