# Bavarian Prealps

Low mountains south of Munich (Tegernsee, Schliersee, Spitzing). Short tours, quick to reach — but only in mid-winter: below about 1,500 m the snow is usually gone by late March ([[spring-snow]]).

Tours: [[hirschberg]] (done), [[brecherspitz]].
