# Moka pot basics (clipped web page)

A moka pot pushes water heated in the lower chamber up through a bed of coffee by steam pressure (about 1–2 bar, far
below espresso's 9 bar). Grind a little finer than for pour-over, fill the basket level without tamping, and start
with water just off the boil so the coffee doesn't cook on the stove. Take the pot off the heat as soon as the flow
turns pale and sputters; cool the base under the tap to stop extraction. Typical ratio: 1:7 to 1:10 (coffee to
water), much stronger than filter coffee.
